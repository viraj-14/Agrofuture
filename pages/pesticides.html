Classification

Types of Pesticides

Understanding the different categories helps choose the right product at the right time.

🦟
Insecticides
Used to control insect pests that damage crops. Available in contact, systemic and fumigant forms.
  • Chlorpyrifos — broad-spectrum
  • Imidacloprid — systemic (sucking pests)
  • Spinosad — bio-based insecticide
  • Malathion — fruit & vegetable pests
🍄
Fungicides
Prevent and treat fungal diseases like blight, rust, mildew and damping-off in crops.
  • Mancozeb — protectant fungicide
  • Propiconazole — systemic action
  • Copper oxychloride — organic option
  • Carbendazim — seed treatment
🌿
Herbicides
Control weeds that compete with crops for nutrients, water, light and space.
  • Glyphosate — non-selective, post-emergence
  • Atrazine — pre-emergence corn weeds
  • 2,4-D — broadleaf weed control
  • Pendimethalin — pre-emergence
🐀
Rodenticides
Used to control rats and rodents that destroy stored grain and field crops.
  • Zinc phosphide — acute rodenticide
  • Bromadiolone — anticoagulant
  • Brodifacoum — second-generation
  • Aluminium phosphide — grain storage
🪱
Nematicides
Protect roots from soil nematodes that reduce plant health and cause massive yield loss.
  • Carbofuran — systemic nematicide
  • Fenamiphos — soil drench
  • Paecilomyces — bio-nematicide
  • Dazomet — soil fumigant
🌱
Biopesticides
Derived from natural organisms — safer for humans, environment, and beneficial insects.
  • Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) — lepidoptera
  • Neem oil — broad-spectrum organic
  • Trichoderma — fungal bio-control
  • Beauveria bassiana — insect pathogen

Safety First

Safe Pesticide Usage

Protect yourself, your family, and your soil. Follow these non-negotiable safety guidelines.

🥽
Wear PPE Always
Gloves, goggles, mask and full-sleeve clothing are mandatory when mixing or spraying any pesticide.
📖
Read the Label
Every label has dosage, dilution, waiting period and first-aid instructions. Never skip reading it.
🕐
Respect Waiting Period
The Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) is critical. Never harvest before the specified number of days after spraying.
💨
Spray in Calm Wind
Spray early morning or late evening when wind speed is below 8 km/h to prevent drift and ensure coverage.
🚰
Safe Disposal
Never dump empty containers near water bodies. Rinse triple-times and puncture before disposal or return to dealer.
🔒
Secure Storage
Store in original containers, in a locked, cool, dry place away from food, feed, and children at all times.
🌧️
Avoid Rain Days
Do not spray if rain is expected within 4–6 hours. Rain washes pesticide off plants and contaminates waterways.
🏥
Know First Aid
In case of poisoning: fresh air, remove contaminated clothing, rinse skin/eyes with water, and go to hospital immediately.
Best Practice

Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

IPM is the gold standard of modern pest control. It combines biological, cultural, physical and chemical tools in a way that minimizes economic, health, and environmental risks.

🌱 IPM Hierarchy

Prevention → Monitoring → Biological Control → Cultural Practices → Mechanical Control → Chemical (last resort) → Evaluation
