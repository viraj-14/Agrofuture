Income Support
PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
₹6,000 / year
Direct income support in 3 equal instalments of ₹2,000 each, transferred directly to bank accounts of eligible farmers every 4 months.
✅ Eligibility Criteria:
  • Small & marginal farmers owning up to 2 hectares
  • Valid Aadhaar card linked to bank account
  • Must be registered in PM-KISAN portal
  • Not a government employee or taxpayer

Crop Insurance
PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
2% Premium (Kharif)
Comprehensive crop insurance scheme covering sowing risk, standing crop damage, post-harvest losses and localised calamities with minimal farmer premium.
✅ Eligibility Criteria:
  • All farmers including tenant farmers
  • Kharif crops: 2% premium, Rabi: 1.5%
  • Horticulture crops: 5% premium max
  • Compulsory for loanee farmers, optional others
Apply Now →
Price Support
PM-AASHA (Price Assurance & Farm Services)
MSP Guarantee
Ensures farmers receive Minimum Support Price for oilseeds and pulses through Price Support Scheme (PSS) and Price Deficiency Payment Scheme (PDPS).
✅ Eligibility Criteria:
  • Farmers growing notified oilseeds & pulses
  • Registered with local APMC or procurement agency
  • Quantity within specified procurement limits
  • Valid land records and identity proof
Apply Now →
Agricultural Credit
Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
Up to ₹3 Lakh @ 4%
Provides timely credit for crop cultivation, post-harvest expenses, maintenance of farm assets and ancillary activities at highly subsidised interest rates.
✅ Eligibility Criteria:
  • Individual/joint farmers owning agricultural land
  • Tenant farmers, oral lessees, share croppers
  • SHGs and Joint Liability Groups of farmers
  • Interest subvention: 3% for prompt repayment
Apply Now →
Digital Agriculture
Digital Agriculture Mission 2021–25
₹2,817 Crore
Building digital infrastructure including Agri Stack, farmers database, unified farmer service interface, and drone technology for precision agriculture.
✅ Key Components:
  • Farmer ID & unified farmers' digital identity
  • Crop survey using drones & satellite
  • Agri market intelligence platform
  • Soil profile mapping for all farm land

Organic Promotion
Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY)
₹50,000 / hectare
Promotes cluster-based organic farming with financial assistance for certification, inputs, marketing and value addition to organic produce groups.
✅ Eligibility Criteria:
  • Minimum 50 farmers forming a cluster
  • Minimum 50 acres of contiguous farmland
  • 3-year commitment to organic conversion
  • PGS-India certification process mandatory
Apply Now →
Irrigation
PM Krishi Sinchai Yojana (PMKSY)
55% subsidy (SC/ST: 75%)
'More Crop Per Drop' — provides subsidy on drip and sprinkler irrigation systems to promote water use efficiency in agriculture across India.
✅ Eligibility Criteria:
  • All categories of farmers
  • Individual, cooperative and FPO farmers
  • SC/ST farmers get additional 20% subsidy
  • Apply through state horticulture department
Apply Now →
Risk Management
Restructured Weather Based Crop Insurance
Weather index-linked
Compensates farmers against weather-related crop losses using automated weather station data — faster claims without field survey requirement.
✅ Eligibility Criteria:
  • Farmers in notified areas and crops
  • Coverage for rainfall, temperature, humidity
  • Automatic trigger-based payout system
  • Available through all commercial banks
Apply Now →
Rural Finance
Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)
₹1 Lakh Crore Fund
Long-term debt financing for post-harvest management infrastructure, community farming assets and agri-logistics projects with 3% interest subvention.
✅ Eligibility Criteria:
  • Farmers, FPOs, PACS, SHGs, Agri-startups
  • Primary agricultural cooperative societies
  • National & state agri federations
  • Maximum ₹2 crore per beneficiary
Apply Now →
History

Key Agricultural
Policy Milestones

India's agricultural policy journey — from Green Revolution to Digital Agriculture.

1966
Green Revolution
HYV wheat & rice seeds transform Indian agriculture — making the country self-sufficient in food grain.
1982
NABARD Established
National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development set up to provide institutional credit to agriculture.
2004
National Agriculture Policy
Farmer Income Doubling target announced. NREGP extended to support agricultural labour.
2016
PM Fasal Bima Yojana
Universal crop insurance launched, replacing existing schemes with lower premiums and faster payouts.
2018
PM-KISAN Launched
₹6,000/year direct income support announced for 140 million small and marginal farming families.
2025
Digital Agriculture Mission
AI, drones, satellite-based precision farming and Agri Stack digital infrastructure rolled out nationally.
