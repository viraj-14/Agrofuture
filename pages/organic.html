Inputs

Natural Fertilizers
& Bio-Inputs

🪱
Vermicompost
Earthworm-processed organic waste. Improves soil texture, water retention and microbial diversity rapidly.
NPK: 2.5–3.0% each
🐄
Farmyard Manure (FYM)
Decomposed dung, urine and straw. Most widely available organic input. Excellent soil conditioner.
NPK: 0.5–0.6–0.5%
🌿
Jeevamrit
ZBNF liquid biofertilizer made from cow dung, urine, jaggery and pulse flour. Activates soil biology rapidly.
Microbial activator
🫘
Neem Cake
Neem seed residue after oil extraction. Provides nitrogen, repels nematodes and soil insects organically.
N: 5.2% + pest control
🦠
Rhizobium Biofertilizer
Nitrogen-fixing bacteria for pulse crops. A single seed treatment fixes up to 150 kg N/ha — eliminating nitrogen fertilizer.
Fixes 80–150 kg N/ha
🌳
Panchagavya
Ancient Indian biostimulant made from 5 cow products. Used as foliar spray to improve growth, immunity and yield.
Growth promoter
🍌
Banana Pseudostem Sap
Rich in potassium and micronutrients. Applied as foliar spray for fruiting crops and high-demand vegetables.
K-rich micronutrient
🌾
Green Manure Crops
Dhaincha, sunhemp, and sesbania grown and incorporated. Add 60–80 kg N/ha and improve soil texture dramatically.
N: 60–80 kg/ha
Go Certified

Get Your Organic Certification

India has two national organic certification systems. Both unlock premium market access, export opportunities, and government subsidies.

PGS-India
Participatory Guarantee System. Community-based, low-cost certification for small farmers. Best for domestic markets and local branding.
NPOP Certified
National Programme for Organic Production. India's official certification for export markets. Recognised by EU, US and Japan.
